# SDE: calfit-mvp

Confirmed by the founder on 2026-10-28.

## Backend (`apps/api`)
- **Endpoints:**
  - `auth/device`;
  - `profile` (GET, PUT, DELETE);
  - `scans`: POST, plus `/confirm`, `/history` (cursor paging), `/stats`, `/{id}` (GET, DELETE), `/{id}/feedback`, `/{id}/serving`;
  - `insights`;
  - `prescriptions` (gated by `PRESCRIPTION_AI_ENABLED`);
  - `health`.
- **Pipeline, scoring, identification, alternatives and observability:** see the architect files.
- **Scripts:**
  - `scripts/run_eval.py`: label-reading accuracy test over labelled photos.
  - `scripts/eval_report.py`: weekly accuracy from user feedback.
  - `scripts/import_india_foods.py` (repo root): rebuilds food.db from Open Food Facts.

## App (`apps/mobile`)
- **Screens:** Home, History, Scan (with product picker), Result, Insights, Profile (body-details sheet, prescription flow), Settings, Onboarding, Privacy policy.
- **Widgets:** score ring, badge and `ScanPhoto` (signed links); scores shown out of 10.
- **Languages:** en, hi, as, bn, kha. Khasi is machine quality.
- **Launcher icons:** generated by `tool/generate_launcher_icons_test.dart`. Play Store icon in `store/`.
